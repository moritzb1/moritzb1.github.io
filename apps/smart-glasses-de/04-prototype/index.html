Core benefits

Your glasses first. Smart glasses second.

No camera. Nothing to explain.

Vistrel has no camera module, not even a switched-off one. A clear "no camera" mark on the frame shows the people around you at a glance. In Germany, 76 % of people do not want to be recorded (Bitkom 2026). With Vistrel, you never become the reason for that worry, whether you are at dinner, in a meeting or visiting friends at home.

Your prescription, your optician, from day one.

Vistrel is planned with prescription lenses fitted in-store by your own optician from launch. You get an eye test, a proper fit and after-sales care from the people who already look after your eyesight. They are your glasses first and smart glasses second.

The information you need, right where you look.

A small heads-up display in one lens shows what matters: the next turn on your route, a message you were waiting for, a live translation, or your speaker notes during a presentation. Your phone stays in your pocket and your attention stays with the people in front of you.

German-speaking, EU-hosted, listening only when you ask.

The assistant understands German and processes on the device wherever possible. Anything that goes to the cloud is hosted in the EU, and we put that promise in writing. The microphone uses push-to-talk by default, and a visible indicator shows when it is listening. You keep control, and everyone around you can see it.
