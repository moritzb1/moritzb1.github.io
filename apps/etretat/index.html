A walk through a Monet

Every surface in this scene, from the chalk cliffs and the sea to the poppies and the sky, is drawn from several hundred thousand oil-paint strokes. Each stroke is a quad fixed in 3D space, and a shader paints its bristles and impasto. There are no textures, no models and no post-filter.

Ride the cliff tops past haystacks and poppy fields, go down to the fishing cove and take the boat through the Porte d’Aval arch, round the Aiguille needle and back.

Each object carries a light and a shadow paint. Distant strokes become fewer and larger and fade out, and quality adapts to your frame rate.
